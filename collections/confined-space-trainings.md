# Confined Space Training — SCORM Courses (29 CFR 1910.146)

Confined spaces — tanks, vaults, pits, silos, manholes and pipelines — are not designed for continuous occupancy and can hide toxic or oxygen-deficient atmospheres, engulfment and entrapment hazards. These SCORM courses train entrants, attendants and entry supervisors on permit-required confined space programs, atmospheric testing, isolation and rescue planning under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.146 and the construction standard, 29 CFR 1926 Subpart AA.

**Regulations:** 29 CFR 1910.146, 29 CFR 1926 Subpart AA
