Musculoskeletal disorders from lifting, repetitive motion and awkward postures are among the most common workplace injuries. OSHA addresses ergonomic hazards under the General Duty Clause. These SCORM courses teach workers and supervisors to recognize ergonomic risk factors and apply practical controls in offices, warehouses and production environments.
