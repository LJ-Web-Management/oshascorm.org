Lithium ion and lithium metal batteries (UN3480, UN3481, UN3090, UN3091) are regulated dangerous goods. Anyone who prepares them for transport must be trained under 49 CFR and, for air shipments, the IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations. These SCORM courses cover classification, packaging, marking, labeling and documentation.

**Regulations:** 49 CFR 173.185, IATA DGR Packing Instructions 965–970
