# Stormwater (SWPPP) Training for Construction — SCORM Courses

> SWPPP training SCORM courses for your LMS: 2 courses from $60.00/seat. Construction sites that disturb one acre or more generally need NPDES permit coverage…

Construction sites that disturb one acre or more generally need NPDES permit coverage and a Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP). These SCORM courses cover SWPPP development and implementation, erosion and sediment control BMPs, inspections and recordkeeping — including Qualified SWPPP Developer (QSD) and Practitioner (QSP) roles.

**Regulations:** EPA NPDES Construction General Permit

## Courses

- [Qualified SWPPP Preparer Training](https://oshascorm.org/products/qualified-swppp-preparer-training) — 7 hours, from $60.00/seat
- [Qualified SWPPP Practitioner (QSP) Training](https://oshascorm.org/products/qualified-swppp-practitioner-qsp-training) — 7 hours, from $60.00/seat

## FAQ

### How are SWPPP training SCORM courses delivered?

Each SWPPP training course is delivered as a SCORM 1.2 or SCORM 2004 ZIP package by email within 24 hours of purchase. Upload it to your LMS, assign learners and track completion and scores.

### How much do SWPPP training courses cost?

This series includes 2 courses, starting from $60.00 per learner seat for a 12-month license. Every course page shows exact pricing for 10 to 200 seats.

### Which regulations do these courses cover?

Courses in this series are aligned with EPA NPDES Construction General Permit, as listed on each course page.
