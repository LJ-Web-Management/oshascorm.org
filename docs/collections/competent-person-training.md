OSHA defines a competent person (29 CFR 1926.32(f)) as someone capable of identifying existing and predictable hazards who has authority to take prompt corrective measures. Many construction standards — excavations, scaffolds, fall protection, cranes and more — require one on site. These SCORM courses build the knowledge supervisors and leads need to fill that role.

**Regulations:** 29 CFR 1926.32(f), 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P, 29 CFR 1926.451
