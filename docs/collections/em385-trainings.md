The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Safety and Health Requirements Manual, EM 385-1-1, governs safety on USACE and many other federal construction projects. These SCORM courses prepare Site Safety and Health Officers (SSHOs), superintendents, quality control managers and crews to meet EM 385-1-1 requirements, from accident prevention plans and activity hazard analyses to fall protection, excavation and equipment safety.
