## FAQ

### How are OSHA construction safety training SCORM courses delivered?

Each OSHA construction safety training course is delivered as a SCORM 1.2 or SCORM 2004 ZIP package by email within 24 hours of purchase. Upload it to your LMS, assign learners and track completion and scores.

### How much do OSHA construction safety training courses cost?

This series includes 40 courses, starting from $6.60 per learner seat for a 12-month license. Every course page shows exact pricing for 10 to 200 seats.

### Which regulations do these courses cover?

Courses in this series are aligned with 29 CFR 1926, as listed on each course page.

### Which LMS platforms support these courses?

Any SCORM-compliant LMS, including Moodle, Canvas, Cornerstone, TalentLMS, Absorb LMS, Blackboard, Docebo, SAP SuccessFactors. Download the free sample package from any course page to test compatibility first.
