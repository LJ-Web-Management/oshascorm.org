## Overview

- Every high-availability data center starts as a set of design decisions — and the wrong ones are expensive to unwind after concrete is poured
- This course walks through the full design lifecycle used by enterprise facility teams: international standards (ANSI/TIA-942, ISO 14644), site selection criteria, thermal design principles, power architecture, and the redundancy trade-offs (N, N+1, N+2, 2N) that determine both uptime and capital cost
- You'll work through real design case studies, thermal modeling concepts, and ROI analysis frameworks used to justify facility investment to executive stakeholders
- Rather than teaching a single vendor's playbook, the course focuses on the trade-off reasoning behind design choices, so you can evaluate proposals, vet vendor recommendations, and defend your own design decisions with data
- 8-hour IACET-accredited online course with interactive knowledge checks and final exam; Printable certificate of completion (no expiration)
