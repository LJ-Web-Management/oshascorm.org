## Overview

- A facility that passes commissioning on paper but fails in production is a commissioning process that missed something
- This course covers the full commissioning lifecycle: pre-commissioning planning, electrical and mechanical systems testing, controls and automation commissioning, and performance baseline establishment
- You'll work with commissioning checklists and test plan templates used on real facility launches, plus staff readiness assessment and vendor coordination — the operational handoff steps that determine whether a new facility actually performs once it's live
- This course is built for the person accountable for a launch date: it gives you a repeatable framework so nothing gets missed between 'construction complete' and 'operationally ready.'
- 8-hour IACET-accredited online course with interactive knowledge checks and final exam; Printable certificate of completion (no expiration)
