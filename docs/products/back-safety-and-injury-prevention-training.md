## Back Safety and Injury Prevention Training

Our Back Safety and Injury Prevention Training is designed in alignment with the guidelines provided by **OSHA** and **NIOSH** for creating injury-free workplaces using ergonomic solutions. The course is also developed to fulfill an employer’s obligation to employees under **OSHA's General Duty Clause**.

The course equips individuals working in any industry and job role with the knowledge and guidance necessary to understand, prevent, and manage back injuries, enhancing overall health and improving workplace efficiency. While back injuries are common, minor pains and aches are often ignored, which can result in chronic back problems. Through detailed instruction, participants learn about the functioning of the back, common types of injuries, risk factors, and causes. The training emphasizes proper lifting techniques, posture maintenance, physical fitness, and stress reduction strategies to mitigate the risk of back injuries. Additionally, the course guides responding to back injuries promptly and appropriately, promoting overall workplace wellness and productivity.
