## CDL Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) – Tanker (N) Endorsement

The CDL Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) Tanker (N) Endorsement course is designed for commercial drivers seeking to operate tank vehicles that transport liquids or gaseous materials in bulk. This training meets the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) requirements under the **ELDT regulations (49 CFR Part 380)** and prepares drivers to safely handle the unique challenges associated with tanker vehicles.

## What is a Tanker Vehicle?

A tanker vehicle is a commercial motor vehicle designed to transport liquids or gases in bulk tanks with an individual rated capacity of more than 119 gallons and an aggregate capacity of 1,000 gallons or more. Operating a tanker requires specialized knowledge due to the movement of liquid cargo and increased rollover risks. Drivers face serious hazards including:

- Liquid surge and sloshing
- Increased stopping distance
- Rollover risks due to shifting center of gravity
- Hazardous material exposure (when applicable)

Improper handling of tanker vehicles can lead to severe crashes, spills, environmental damage, and injuries, making proper training critical for driver and public safety.

## Course Purpose

This ELDT Tanker (N) Endorsement Training prepares drivers to:

- Understand tanker vehicle design and inspection requirements
- Recognize the effects of liquid surge and load shift
- Apply safe driving techniques specific to tank vehicles
- Prevent rollovers and loss-of-control incidents
- Follow FMCSA safety regulations for tanker operations

## Employer Responsibilities

Under FMCSA’s **Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) regulations**, employers must ensure that drivers seeking a Tanker (N) endorsement complete training from a compliant provider listed on the FMCSA Training Provider Registry (TPR). Employers are responsible for confirming drivers are properly trained and qualified before operating tanker vehicles.

### Course Objective

Upon completion of the CDL Entry Level Driver Training (ELDT) Tanker (N) Endorsement, participants will achieve the following learning objectives:

- Gain knowledge of tanker vehicle regulations and endorsement requirements.
- Inspect tank vehicles for structural integrity, leaks, valves, and safety equipment before operation.
- Explain the effects of liquid surge and a high center of gravity on vehicle stability.
- Demonstrate proper tank loading procedures, including calculating outage (ullage).
- Apply safe driving techniques on curves, ramps, and slippery road conditions.
- Identify different types of tank vehicles (baffled, bulkhead, smoothbore) and understand their handling characteristics.
- Recognize and respond to emergency situations, including brake failure or vehicle instability.
