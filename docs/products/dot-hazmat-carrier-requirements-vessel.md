## Course Overview

The **DOT Hazmat Carrier Requirements: Vessel** course provides targeted guidance for employers and employees engaged in the transportation of hazardous materials by vessel. The course covers carrier responsibilities, vessel-specific requirements, safe cargo handling, stowage, segregation, and documentation needed to comply with U.S. regulations and to support safe maritime transport.

### Who should take this course?

- Personnel responsible for packaging, labeling, loading, and unloading hazardous materials for vessel transport
- Vessel operators, cargo supervisors, and stevedores
- Safety and compliance officers overseeing marine hazmat shipments
- Shippers and freight forwarders coordinating vessel transport

### Learning Objectives

- Explain carrier responsibilities and vessel-specific hazmat regulations.
- Identify proper stowage, segregation, and securing methods for hazardous materials aboard vessels.
- Understand documentation requirements, including bills of lading and dangerous goods declarations where applicable.
- Recognize package, label, and placard requirements specific to vessel transport.
- Apply safe loading/unloading procedures, emergency response actions, and reporting obligations.

### Course Outline

1. Regulatory context: DOT requirements and applicable maritime regulations
2. Carrier duties and documentation for vessel shipments
3. Stowage, segregation, and securing hazardous cargo
4. Packaging, labeling, and placarding considerations for vessel transport
5. Loading/unloading procedures and handling hazardous materials safely
6. Emergency response, spill control, and reporting incidents
7. Practical scenarios and knowledge check

### Prerequisites

Learners should have basic hazmat knowledge, preferably from DOT Hazmat Transportation: General Awareness Training, before taking this vessel-specific course.

### Course Objective

This DOT Hazmat Carrier Requirements: Vessel course equips you with the knowledge and skills to navigate the world of transporting hazardous materials by ship. Upon completion, you'll be able to:

- Gain a strong understanding of the regulatory framework governing the transport of hazardous materials by vessel, ensuring compliance with 49 CFR Part 176 (Carriage by Vessel) of the Hazardous Materials Regulations (HMR).
- Explain the basic differences between the International Maritime Dangerous Goods (IMDG) code's Dangerous Goods List and the HMR's Hazardous Materials Table, ensuring accurate classification of materials for maritime transport.
- Describe the critical shipping documentation that must accompany hazardous materials offered for transport by vessel, guaranteeing clear communication and compliance throughout the journey.
- Understand the general and specific requirements for safely segregating and stowing hazardous materials on ships, minimizing the risk of accidents and incidents.
- Grasp the procedures for handling leaking and damaged hazardous material packages onboard vessels, ensuring the safety of crew and cargo.
- Summarize the reporting protocols for incidents involving hazardous materials on ships, ensuring timely communication with authorities in case of an emergency.
