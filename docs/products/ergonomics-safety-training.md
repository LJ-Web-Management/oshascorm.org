### Overview

## Ergonomics Safety Training

The **Ergonomics Safety Training course** is designed to empower employers and employees to build safer, healthier, and more productive work environments. This course follows OSHA and NIOSH ergonomics guidelines, as well as OSHA’s **General Duty Clause**, which requires employers to provide workplaces free from recognized hazards. Through evidence-based ergonomic strategies, this training helps reduce **Work-Related Musculoskeletal Disorders (WMSDs)** and other ergonomic-related injuries that commonly occur in office, industrial, and field operations.

Participants will learn how to evaluate physical demands, recognize early signs of discomfort, implement proper workstation setup, and apply ergonomic controls to reduce strain and injury risks. This training emphasizes **proactive prevention strategies** such as hazard identification, early intervention, and workplace design improvements to support long-term employee well-being.

### What Learners Will Gain

- Understanding of ergonomics principles and their importance in workplace safety.
- Insight into common causes and risk factors of musculoskeletal disorders (MSDs).
- Knowledge of OSHA and NIOSH guidelines related to ergonomics and injury prevention.
- Skills to assess job tasks and identify ergonomic hazards in both office and industrial settings.
- Ability to implement engineering and administrative controls to reduce ergonomic risks.
- Techniques for setting up ergonomic-friendly workstations and tools.
- Strategies for developing and promoting ergonomic programs within the organization.
- Understanding of early reporting procedures to prevent minor issues from becoming serious injuries.

### Who Can Benefit

Ergonomic risks exist across all industries, making this training valuable for **all employees**. It is particularly useful for individuals responsible for workplace safety, productivity, and employee well-being, including facility managers, safety professionals, engineers, maintenance staff, HR personnel, ergonomics specialists, supervisors, and both office and industrial workers.

Completing this course equips participants with the tools needed to create ergonomic-friendly environments that reduce injuries, increase comfort, improve productivity, and support long-term workforce wellness.

### Course Objective

By taking this Ergonomics Safety Training course, you'll gain the knowledge and skills to:

- Define ergonomics and understand its crucial role in creating a safe and healthy workspace.
- Recognize common musculoskeletal disorders (MSDs) and the factors that cause them, focusing on work-related issues (WMSDs).
- Understand OSHA's perspective on ergonomics hazards and the regulations in place to prevent them.
- Learn and implement proactive strategies to prevent ergonomic issues before they arise.
- Analyze the health risks associated with ergonomic hazards and how they can impact your well-being.
- Grasp the role of ergonomics programs in creating a culture of safety and fostering continuous improvement in the workplace.
- Develop and implement an effective ergonomics program to address potential risks and create a comfortable work environment.
- Apply the hierarchy of controls to identify and mitigate ergonomic hazards at their source, preventing injuries and promoting safety.
