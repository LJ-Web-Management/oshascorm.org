### Overview

## Hazardous Waste Manifest Training

Learn to prepare, submit, track, and correct EPA hazardous waste manifests (Form 8700-22) and use the e-Manifest system in compliance with **49 CFR 172 Subpart H**.

## Course Overview

This **Hazardous Waste Manifest Training** provides practical instruction for hazardous waste generators, transporters, and TSDF personnel on completing the EPA Manifest (Form 8700-22), navigating the e-Manifest system, resolving common discrepancies, and maintaining accurate documentation. The course emphasizes compliance with federal regulations and real-world scenarios to ensure manifests are completed and tracked correctly.

## What You Will Learn

- How to complete all sections of EPA Form 8700-22 (Hazardous Waste Manifest).
- How to register for and use the e-Manifest system to create, submit, and track manifests electronically.
- How to select proper waste codes, describe waste accurately, and assign generator/transporter/TSDF information.
- How to identify and resolve common manifest discrepancies (incorrect waste codes, missing signatures, routing errors).
- Procedures for manifest corrections, voids, and obtaining proper documentation for chain-of-custody.
- Recordkeeping requirements and retention best practices to meet EPA and state regulations.
- Roles and responsibilities of generators, transporters, and TSDFs under 49 CFR 172 Subpart H.

## Who Should Attend

- **Hazardous Waste Generators:** LQGs and SQGs, EHS staff, facility managers responsible for shipments.
- **Transporters:** Drivers, dispatch, and logistics personnel handling hazardous waste shipments.
- **TSDF Personnel:** Receiving clerks, waste handlers, compliance officers, and recordkeeping staff.
- **Environmental Consultants & Compliance Officers:** Auditors and advisors ensuring manifest compliance.
- **Laboratory & Industrial Staff:** Personnel who generate hazardous waste in labs or production.
- **Regulatory Inspectors & Emergency Responders:** EPA and state inspectors or responders needing manifest knowledge.

 **Regulatory Note:** This course is aligned with federal manifest requirements in **49 CFR 172 Subpart H**. State-level requirements may vary; participants should verify additional state rules that apply to manifesting and e-Manifest submissions.

### Course Objective

- Identify key environmental regulations related to the generation, transportation, and disposal of hazardous waste.
- Explain the purpose and use of the Uniform Hazardous Waste Manifest in compliance with EPA and DOT standards.
- Summarize the roles and responsibilities of personnel involved in preparing and signing hazardous waste manifests.
- Describe the steps involved in properly completing and tracking a hazardous waste manifest.
- Define the importance of accurate waste classification and labeling.
- Explain the documentation and recordkeeping requirements associated with hazardous waste shipments.
- Discuss the importance of proper training and communication in preventing transportation-related incidents.
- Recognize the connection between manifest compliance and overall environmental safety and legal accountability.
