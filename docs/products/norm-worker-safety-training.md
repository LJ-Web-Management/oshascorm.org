## NORM Worker Safety Training

Our NORM (Naturally Occurring Radioactive Material) Worker Safety Training course is designed to provide workers with essential safety knowledge for identifying, understanding, and safely working in environments where NORM may be present. The training focuses on recognizing potential radiation hazards, following safe work practices, using appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE), and minimizing occupational exposure to radioactive materials.

## Course Overview

The NORM Worker Safety Training prepares workers to recognize and control common hazards associated with Naturally Occurring Radioactive Material (NORM) in the workplace. Students will learn about:

- Understanding NORM and TENORM
- Potential health and safety hazards associated with NORM
- Radiation exposure and basic radiation protection principles
- Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Safe work practices and contamination control
- Monitoring and detection of radioactive materials
- Decontamination procedures
- Proper handling, storage, and disposal practices
- Emergency response procedures
- And more

## Who Should Enroll?

This course is intended for workers who may encounter Naturally Occurring Radioactive Material (NORM) or Technologically Enhanced Naturally Occurring Radioactive Material (TENORM) as part of their job duties. It is particularly relevant for employees working in industries where NORM contamination may occur, including:

- Oil and gas workers
- Pipeline and maintenance personnel
- Environmental and remediation workers
- Waste management personnel
- Industrial and maintenance workers
- Workers involved in NORM-contaminated equipment handling
- Supervisors and managers overseeing NORM-related work

Employers should evaluate their specific operations, potential radiation hazards, and applicable federal, state, and local requirements to determine the appropriate level of NORM safety training for their employees.

### Course Objective

Upon completing the NORM Worker Safety Training, learners will be able to:

- Define **Naturally Occurring Radioactive Material (NORM)** and its key characteristics.
- Explain the fundamentals of **ionizing radiation** and **radioactive decay**.
- Interpret common **radiation units**, **prefixes**, and **basic dose calculations**.
- Describe the **biological effects** of **radiation exposure** and the **factors** that influence **risk**.
- Identify the **regulatory requirements** and **industry standards** applicable to NORM.
- Recognize **industries**, **processes**, and **activities** where NORM may be encountered.
- Identify **common NORM hazards** and **potential exposure pathways** in the workplace.
- Explain the principles of **radiation monitoring**, **measurement**, and **NORM characterization**.
- Describe **environmental monitoring** methods used to assess **radiological contamination**.
- Apply the principles of **radiation protection**, including **time**, **distance**, and **shielding**.
- Describe the components of an effective **Radiation Protection Program**.
- Select appropriate **respiratory protection** and **personal protective equipment (PPE)** for NORM work.
- Describe the **principles** and **best practices** for the **safe management** of **bulk NORM residues** and **waste**.
- Demonstrate **safe work practices**, including **decontamination**, **restricted area controls**, **frisking**, and **documentation**.
- Recognize the **hazards associated** with **legacy NORM sites**, **transportation**, and specialized **mining** and **processing operations**.
- Explain **emergency response procedures** and **regulatory compliance requirements** for NORM incidents.
