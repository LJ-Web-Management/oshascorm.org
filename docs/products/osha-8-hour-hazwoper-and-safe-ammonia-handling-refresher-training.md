### Overview

## OSHA 8-Hour HAZWOPER & Safe Ammonia Handling Refresher Training

Annual refresher training for employees handling hazardous substances and ammonia in industrial settings, meeting OSHA standards 29 CFR 1910.120 and 29 CFR 1926.65.

## Course Overview

This 8-hour refresher course is designed for workers who handle hazardous substances and respond to hazardous waste operations, particularly those working with ammonia. The training provides specialized guidance on safely handling ammonia, a highly hazardous chemical commonly used in refrigeration, manufacturing, and other industries. It fulfills the annual refresher requirement under OSHA's HAZWOPER standards.

## Who Can Benefit

This course is intended for employees who have completed the initial 24-hour or 40-hour HAZWOPER training and require annual refresher training. Ideal participants include:

- Hazardous materials handlers
- Emergency response personnel
- Industrial refrigeration workers
- Waste management professionals
- Maintenance staff handling ammonia

### Course Objective

## OSHA 8-Hour HAZWOPER and Safe Ammonia Handling Refresher Training

This training program equips you with the critical knowledge and skills to safely handle hazardous substances, particularly ammonia, while maintaining compliance with OSHA regulations. Upon completion, you will be able to:
