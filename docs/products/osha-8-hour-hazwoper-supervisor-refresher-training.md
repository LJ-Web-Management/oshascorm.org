## OSHA HAZWOPER 8 Hour Supervisor Refresher Training

This OSHA HAZWOPER 8 Hour Supervisor Refresher Training is specifically designed for managers and supervisors who have previously completed their initial 24-hour or 40-hour HAZWOPER training, as well as the initial 8-hour OSHA HAZWOPER Supervisor Training. The course ensures continued compliance with OSHA regulations **29 CFR 1910.120(e)(8)** and **29 CFR 1926.65(e)(8)**.

## Course Overview

The course refreshes key knowledge and skills required for supervisors overseeing hazardous waste operations and emergency response scenarios. Participants will gain a deeper understanding of toxicology, hazard classification, decontamination procedures, personal protective equipment (PPE), and effective emergency response protocols.

### Course Objective

Upon completion of the OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Supervisor Refresher Training course, the learner will be able to:

- Understand fully the OSHA regulations and requirements pertaining to general site workers.
- Comprehend the fundamental principles of toxicology and their relevance to chemical exposure.
- Understand the site control strategies and operations performed in each work zone.
- Summarize the principles of decontamination and the methods used to decontaminate personnel and equipment.
- Recognize the importance of personal protective equipment (PPE), and identify and select appropriate PPE for specific worksites.
- Identify physical, biological, chemical, and environmental hazards present at hazardous waste sites.
- Recognize hazardous waste site emergencies and understand the appropriate response procedures.
- Outline the monitoring and detection tools available to workers at hazardous waste sites.
