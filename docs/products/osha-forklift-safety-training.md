## OSHA Forklift Safety Training

The OSHA Forklift Safety Training course is designed to enhance workplace safety when using powered industrial trucks such as forklifts. This training covers the essential requirements of OSHA’s General Industry Standard **29 CFR 1910.178** and Construction Industry Standard **29 CFR 1926.602(d)**.

Participants will learn safe operating procedures, pre-operation inspections, load handling techniques, stability and tipping principles, hazard recognition, and emergency response measures. The goal is to ensure forklift operators and nearby workers remain safe while also helping employers meet OSHA compliance requirements.

### Why This Course Is Important

Forklifts are involved in thousands of workplace injuries every year, many of which result from improper use, lack of knowledge, or failure to follow safety protocols. This course is important because:

- OSHA requires forklift operators to receive proper training and evaluation.
- Improper forklift use can lead to serious injuries, fatalities, and costly property damage.
- Employers may face penalties for non-compliance with OSHA standards.
- Training reduces risks, enhances productivity, and builds a strong safety culture.
- It ensures that operators understand equipment limitations and safe practices.

### Who Should Take This Course?

This forklift safety training is ideal for:

- New and experienced forklift operators.
- Warehouse and distribution center workers.
- Construction site personnel involved in material handling.
- Safety managers, supervisors, and team leads overseeing forklift operations.
- Employers seeking OSHA-compliant training for their workforce.
- Maintenance staff responsible for servicing forklift equipment.

### What This Course Covers

The training provides a thorough understanding of forklift operation and OSHA compliance, including:

- OSHA requirements for powered industrial trucks (29 CFR 1910.178 & 1926.602).
- Types of forklifts and their designated uses.
- Pre-operation inspections and routine maintenance checks.
- Load handling, lifting, transporting, and stacking techniques.
- Stability triangle, load center, and avoiding tip-overs.
- Hazard recognition in warehouses, loading docks, and construction sites.
- Safe travel practices, pedestrian awareness, and workplace communication.
- Parking, shutdown procedures, and unattended vehicle rules.
- Emergency procedures and accident prevention strategies.
- Employer responsibilities and documentation requirements.

### Course Objective

By taking this course, you'll become a forklift pro! You'll be able to:

- Recognize different forklifts and their safe operation.
- Learn to identify and avoid dangers while operating a forklift.
- Understand OSHA regulations (it's the law!) for safe forklift use.

- Learn basic maintenance tasks to keep your forklift in top shape.
