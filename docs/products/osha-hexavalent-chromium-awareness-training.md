## OSHA Hexavalent Chromium (Cr(VI)) Awareness Training

Hexavalent chromium, also known as **chromium VI or Cr(VI)**, is a hazardous chemical compound that poses serious health risks, including cancer and respiratory issues. Workers in various industries may encounter this compound during processes involving chromium metal or its compounds, such as *chroming, plating, melting, welding, and manufacturing of paints or pigments*. Chromium is a common element found in everyday products, from shiny metals to paints, making awareness and safety training essential.

The **OSHA Hexavalent Chromium Awareness Training** is designed to educate workers on the hazards associated with Cr(VI) exposure, help them identify potential sources, and implement proper safety practices. This training equips both employees and employers with the knowledge needed to reduce health risks and comply with OSHA standards.

### What You Will Learn

- Introduction to hexavalent chromium and its common industrial uses
- Health risks associated with exposure, including cancer, respiratory diseases, and skin irritation
- Potential sources of hexavalent chromium in the workplace
- OSHA standards and regulations for Cr(VI) in general and construction industries
- Preventative measures to reduce exposure, including PPE, engineering controls, and safe work practices
- Proper handling, storage, and disposal of materials containing hexavalent chromium
- Emergency response and first aid procedures for exposure incidents
- Monitoring, recordkeeping, and compliance requirements under OSHA

### Who Needs This Training?

Any personnel working in environments where hexavalent chromium is present or could be encountered as part of their job should take this awareness training. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Manufacturing workers handling chromium-containing materials
- Plating and finishing operators
- Welders, painters, and coating personnel
- Maintenance and cleaning staff exposed to Cr(VI)
- Supervisors and safety officers responsible for employee protection
- Contractors and subcontractors entering areas where Cr(VI) may be present

### Regulatory Compliance

Training ensures compliance with OSHA’s Hexavalent Chromium Standards: **General Industry:** 29 CFR 1910 Subpart Z, 1910.1026 (Chromium VI) **Construction Industry:** 29 CFR 1926 Subpart Z, 1926.1126 (Chromium VI)
