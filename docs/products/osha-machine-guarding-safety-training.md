## OSHA Machine Guarding Safety Training

**Machinery is essential in countless industries**, but without proper safeguards, it can become a major safety hazard. Our OSHA Machine Guarding Safety Training equips operators, supervisors, and safety teams with the knowledge needed to prevent accidents involving moving parts, rotating components, and unexpected machine activation.

This self-paced online course follows OSHA’s Machine Guarding Standard **(29 CFR 1910 Subpart O)** and focuses on identifying hazards, choosing the right guards, maintaining machinery, and applying safe operating procedures.

## Course Overview

Whether you're operating machinery, supervising work on the shop floor, or managing facility compliance, this course teaches you how to reduce injuries and violations through proper guarding practices and hazard controls.

### Why Choose Our OSHA Machine Guarding Safety Training?

 **Compliance-driven** — Meets OSHA standards outlined in *29 CFR 1910 Subpart O*. **Easy-to-understand safeguards** — Step-by-step guidance on selecting guards and preventing key injuries such as amputations, lacerations, and crush hazards. **Online & self-paced** — Employees can train on any device, anytime. **Designed by safety experts** — Real-world examples show how to manage mechanical hazards effectively. **Certificate included** — Valid for OSHA documentation and proof of compliance.

### What You Will Learn

- Types of machinery hazards and how to detect them
- OSHA requirements for machine safeguarding (29 CFR 1910 Subpart O)
- Purpose and function of fixed, interlocked, adjustable, and self-adjusting guards
- Inspection, maintenance, and safe machine operation procedures
- Employer and employee roles in machine safety
- Basic lockout/tagout concepts and its relation to machine guarding
- How to identify and report unsafe or unguarded conditions
- Emergency steps and responsibilities during machine-related incidents
- Ongoing safety practices for preventing future hazards

### Who Needs This Course?

- Machine operators and service technicians (industry & construction)
- Foremen supervising machinery use
- Safety officers and compliance personnel
- Plant and equipment managers reducing injury risks
- Maintenance crews working on energized or moving machinery
- Employees setting up, running, or cleaning machinery
- New hires working around safeguarded equipment

### Course Objective

The **OSHA Machine Guarding Safety Training** equips you with the knowledge and skills to ensure workplace safety when operating or maintaining machinery. Upon completion, you will be able to:

- Understand OSHA's machine guarding standards and their applicability, ensuring compliance with federal safety regulations.
- Identify common machine hazards and the risks associated with moving parts, helping to prevent workplace injuries.
- Recognize different types of machine guards and safety devices, learning how they protect workers and enhance operational safety.
- Understand employer and employee responsibilities in machine safety, ensuring a culture of compliance and risk prevention.
- Learn best practices for inspecting, maintaining, and implementing effective machine guarding measures to promote a safe work environment.
