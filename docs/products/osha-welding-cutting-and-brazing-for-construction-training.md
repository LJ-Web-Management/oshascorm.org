### Overview

## OSHA Welding, Cutting & Brazing for Construction Training

Awareness-level training for construction workers covering hazards, safe work practices, PPE, fire safety, ventilation, and regulatory requirements for welding, cutting, and brazing operations.

## Course Overview

The **OSHA Welding, Cutting & Brazing for Construction** course provides workers with the knowledge needed to recognize and control hazards associated with welding, cutting, and brazing. Topics include toxic fumes, burns, electrical risks, eye injuries, fire hazards, gas cylinder safety, equipment inspection, and ventilation practices.

## Who Should Enroll?

- Welders, cutters, and brazers working on construction sites
- Maintenance personnel and fabricators who perform hot work
- Supervisors, safety officers, and site leads overseeing hot-work operations
- Workers who may be exposed to fumes, sparks, or heat from nearby operations

## What You'll Learn

- Hazards associated with welding, cutting, and brazing (fumes, heat, sparks, electricity)
- Selection and correct use of PPE — helmets, gloves, goggles, and protective clothing
- Safe handling, storage, and use of gas cylinders and fuel sources
- Pre-use inspection, maintenance, and safe operation of welding and cutting equipment
- Fire prevention: assessing flammability, using fire extinguishers, and implementing fire-watch procedures
- Ventilation techniques to control exposure to hazardous fumes and gases
- Good housekeeping and work practices to minimize risks and maintain a safe workspace
- When to stop work and escalate safety concerns to supervisors or safety personnel

## Regulatory References

Welding, cutting, and brazing activities are addressed in OSHA standards including:

- **General Industry:** 29 CFR 1910 Subparts I, Q, and Z
- **Construction:** 29 CFR 1926 Subparts J and Z
- **Maritime:** 29 CFR 1915 Subparts D, I, G, P, and Z

### Course Objective

By the end of this course, you'll be able to:

- Understand the risks involved with oxyacetylene torches and the precautions needed for safe use.
- Learn how to properly and safely handle welding gas cylinders.
- Identify potential dangers associated with arc welding and the corresponding safety measures.
- Grasp the importance of personal protective equipment (PPE) for hot work activities.
- Conduct thorough inspections and preparations of hot work areas before starting any tasks.
- Implement preventive measures to prevent fires before welding or cutting operations begin.
- Follow appropriate safety protocols when welding or cutting containers.
- Understand the dangers of breathing in welding fumes and discover effective strategies to keep them at bay.
- Understand the potential hazards of radiation and light exposure during hot work processes.
