## Overview

- The building envelope — walls, roof, insulation, and air sealing — determines how hard your cooling system has to work before a single CRAC unit turns on
- This course covers building envelope fundamentals: insulation performance, air sealing, moisture control, and how envelope condition directly affects PUE and cooling cost
- You'll leave able to spot envelope deficiencies that are quietly driving up your energy bill before they show up as a bigger mechanical problem
- 2-hour IACET-accredited online course with interactive knowledge checks and final exam; Printable certificate of completion (no expiration)
