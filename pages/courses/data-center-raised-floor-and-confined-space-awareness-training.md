## Overview

- The space under a raised floor is a confined space with its own hazards — cabling, airflow obstructions, and limited visibility
- This awareness-level course covers safe raised-floor work practices and the confined space fundamentals relevant to under-floor access
- You'll leave knowing exactly when under-floor work crosses the line into permit-required confined space territory
- 1-hour IACET-accredited online course with interactive knowledge checks and final exam; Printable certificate of completion (valid 1 year)
