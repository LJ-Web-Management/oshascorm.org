## Overview

- UPS and battery rooms carry hazards beyond standard electrical work: battery chemistry, off-gassing, and stored energy that remains even when a system is powered down
- This course covers the safety practices specific to working around UPS and battery installations
- You'll leave understanding the PPE and ventilation requirements that go beyond standard electrical safety, and how to respond if something goes wrong
- 2-hour IACET-accredited online course with interactive knowledge checks and final exam; Printable certificate of completion (valid 1 year)
