## Overview

- A facility is only as observable as its monitoring strategy — and most downtime incidents were technically visible in the data well before they became outages
- This course covers sensor deployment and calibration, network monitoring fundamentals, BMS architecture, data analytics and predictive modeling, and dashboard design
- Hands-on labs cover monitoring tool training, data visualization, and alert tuning — because a monitoring system that generates too many false alerts gets ignored, and one that's too quiet misses real problems
- You'll leave with a monitoring strategy framework built to reduce downtime, not just generate reports: the right sensors, the right thresholds, and dashboards that surface what actually matters
