## Overview

- Power failures are the fastest way to take a data center offline, which is why electrical infrastructure gets more redundancy scrutiny than almost any other system
- This course covers the full power chain: utility interconnection, switchgear and transformers, UPS systems (topologies, battery management, maintenance), PDU architecture, and redundancy strategy
- Electrical safety and maintenance best practices run through every module — this isn't just architecture theory, it's paired with equipment evaluation frameworks, troubleshooting methodologies, and maintenance scheduling you can apply on the floor
- You'll finish with a working understanding of electrical architecture trade-offs, so you can evaluate vendor proposals, troubleshoot power issues faster, and optimize your facility's power chain for both reliability and efficiency
- 8-hour IACET-accredited online course with interactive knowledge checks and final exam; Printable certificate of completion (no expiration)
