## Overview

- The TIER classification system (I through IV) is the industry's shorthand for facility reliability — but understanding what actually separates a TIER III facility from a TIER IV one takes more than reading a definitions page
- This course breaks down the topology requirements, electrical redundancy, cooling architecture, and management systems behind each TIER level
- You'll learn the assessment methodologies used to audit an existing facility against TIER benchmarks, including gap analysis procedures and infrastructure scoring methods — the same frameworks used before a formal certification engagement, without the certification requirement or cost
- This is practical benchmarking knowledge: understand where your facility sits today, what it would take to move up a TIER level, and how to communicate that gap to stakeholders
- 8-hour IACET-accredited online course with interactive knowledge checks and final exam; Printable certificate of completion (no expiration)
