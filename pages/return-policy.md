# Return Policy

> Not sure if you or your team can complete the course? We've got you covered. You get a full money-back guarantee within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days…

Not sure if you or your team can complete the course? We've got you covered.

You get a full money-back guarantee within **30 days of purchase**. After 30 days, we'll issue a credit for the full amount paid toward your next purchase, valid for 12 months.

To request a refund or credit, email [info@oshascorm.org](mailto:info@oshascorm.org) or call [1-866-429-6742](tel:1-866-429-6742) with your order number. We'll confirm receipt within 2 business days and process approved requests within 3-5 business days, to the original payment method where possible.

## Renewals

Course licenses do not renew automatically. Before your license expires, we'll email you a renewal notice. Nothing is charged until you place a new order.

## Our Guarantee

**Guaranteed delivery.** Standard SCORM packages are delivered within 24 hours of successful payment. Custom-branded (white-label) packages are delivered within 7 business days.

[**Guaranteed value.** If you find the identical course package offered elsewhere at a lower price within 30 days of purchase, send us the listing and we'll refund the difference.](/)

**Guaranteed satisfaction.** If you're not satisfied, notify us in writing and we'll work with you to make it right, subject to the terms in the Return Policy above.

**Technical support.** Free technical support for installation and performance issues is included for 12 months from purchase. Call [1-866-429-6742](tel:1-866-429-6742), Monday–Friday, 7:00 AM–5:00 PM CST, or email [info@oshascorm.org](mailto:info@oshascorm.org).
