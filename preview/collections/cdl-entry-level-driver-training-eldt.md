Since February 7, 2022, FMCSA requires Entry-Level Driver Training before a first-time Class A or Class B CDL, a CDL upgrade, or a passenger (P), school bus (S) or hazardous materials (H) endorsement. These SCORM courses deliver the ELDT theory curriculum defined in 49 CFR Part 380, Subpart F so training providers and fleets listed on the FMCSA Training Provider Registry can deliver it in their own LMS.

**Regulations:** 49 CFR Part 380, Subpart F

## FAQ

### How are ELDT theory training SCORM courses delivered?

Each ELDT theory training course is delivered as a SCORM 1.2 or SCORM 2004 ZIP package by email within 24 hours of purchase. Upload it to your LMS, assign learners and track completion and scores.

### How much do ELDT theory training courses cost?

This series includes 8 courses, starting from $109.99 per learner seat for a 12-month license. Every course page shows exact pricing for 10 to 200 seats.

### Which regulations do these courses cover?

Courses in this series are aligned with 49 CFR Part 380, Subpart F, as listed on each course page.

### Which LMS platforms support these courses?

Any SCORM-compliant LMS, including Moodle, Canvas, Cornerstone, TalentLMS, Absorb LMS, Blackboard, Docebo, SAP SuccessFactors. Download the free sample package from any course page to test compatibility first.
