# About OSHASCORM.org

> IACET-accredited, OSHA-aligned SCORM safety training for employers, training providers and LMS operators — backed by our 360° Guarantee®.

OSHASCORM.org operates as a subsidiary of [Industrial Certified Training, LLC](https://ictraining.us/), a company specializing in workplace safety training, and partners with [HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC](https://hazwoper-osha.com/) to provide a comprehensive selection of OSHA compliance courses. ## How courses are built and maintained

- Each course page lists the regulation or standard it is aligned with (for example 29 CFR 1910.120 for HAZWOPER or NFPA 70E for electrical safety).
- Courses include knowledge checks, a final exam and a certificate of completion to support recordkeeping and audits.
- We regularly review OSHA, DOT, EPA and other state and federal regulations and update our courses to reflect changes. An optional annual maintenance plan delivers those updates to your LMS automatically — see the [FAQ](/pages/faq).
