Grounding and Bonding Fundamentals — course overview

Grounding and Bonding Fundamentals is an online safety training course delivered as an LMS-ready SCORM package, aligned with N/A (Voluntary/Technical Skills) – No specific regulation - foundational trade skill / NFPA 70 (NEC) informative. It is part of our Technical Trade Skills - Electrical training library.

What this course covers

  • Core concepts of grounding and bonding for electrical contractors, facilities maintenance
  • Operating principles, components and system terminology
  • Start-up, normal operation, monitoring and shutdown procedures
  • Preventive maintenance, inspection and common troubleshooting
  • Safety precautions and applicable codes/standards
  • 2-hour IACET-accredited online course with interactive knowledge checks and final exam; Printable certificate of completion (no expiration)
