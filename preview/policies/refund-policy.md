# Refund policy

> At OSHASCORM.ORG, we want you to know that your purchase is 100% risk-free. We are confident enough in the strength of our training to offer a 30-Day…

At OSHASCORM.ORG, we want you to know that your purchase is **100% risk-free**. We are confident enough in the strength of our training to offer a **30-Day Money-Back Guarantee**.

In the rare event that you are unsatisfied with the value of our training, simply notify us in writing at [info@oshascorm.org](mailto:info@oshascorm.org) with a valid reason. We shall promptly refund your payment. Most refunds are returned via the original payment method. Please note that certain restrictions may apply, and some purchases may only be eligible for credit refunds.

Please note that no refunds will be issued after 30 days from the date of purchase.

## Be certain with our 360° Guarantee®

You're covered from every angle.

### Guaranteed delivery

We promise on-time delivery within 24 hours after successful payment. If we miss the deadline, your order is free.

### Guaranteed value

If you find a lower price elsewhere within 30 days of purchase, let us know, and we'll refund the difference and then some.

### Guaranteed happiness

If you're not 100% satisfied with your order, we'll work with you to make it right or refund your money—your choice. Note that you must provide notice of cancellation in order to receive a refund, credit your account, or to cancel the invoice.

### Technical Support

We offer free technical support for up to one year from the day of purchase. Our support team is here to assist you with any issues or questions you may have.

Just call us at **1-866-429-6742** between 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM CST to notify us.
