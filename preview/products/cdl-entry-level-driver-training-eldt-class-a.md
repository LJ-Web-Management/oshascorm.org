## Overview

### Overview

## CDL Entry Level Driver Training (ELDT) – Class A

Our **CDL Entry Level Driver Training (ELDT) – Class A** course provides comprehensive training for commercial drivers to safely operate long combination vehicles. This program aligns with Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) and DOT regulations under 49 CFR Part 380. Participants gain essential skills in route planning, emergency response, and compliance with safety regulations.

## Key Features of Class A CDL Training

- Basic operation of a commercial motor vehicle.
- Safe driving practices for long combination vehicles.
- Vehicle inspection and maintenance procedures.
- Use of electronic logging devices and other relevant technology.
- Hours-of-service regulations and fatigue management.

## Why Choose Our CDL Class A Training?

- **FMCSA Compliant:** Training fully complies with FMCSA regulations as of February 2022 for ELDT Class A CDL.
- **Comprehensive & Practical:** Covers all aspects of safe operation, emergency protocols, and vehicle handling.
- **Suitable for New Drivers:** Designed for driver-trainees seeking a Class A CDL for the first time.
- **Flexible Validity:** ELDT Class A training does not expire, but CDL must be renewed every five years, with state-specific requirements possible.

  Note: Completion of this course fulfills FMCSA’s ELDT requirement before taking the knowledge test to obtain your Class A CDL license.

### Course Objective

## CDL Entry Level Driver Training (ELDT) - Class A Drivers

This CDL Entry Level Driver Training (ELDT) - Class A Drivers course equips you with the knowledge and skills required to excel as a professional commercial driver. By the end, you'll be prepared to:

- Understand Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations (FMCSR) and commercial vehicle safety requirements, ensuring legal and responsible operation.
- Develop proficiency in pre-trip, en-route, and post-trip inspections, focusing on critical vehicle components like brakes, tires, and coupling devices.
- Learn safe driving techniques for handling large vehicles, including turning, backing, and navigating tight spaces.
- Master the safe operation of combination vehicles, ensuring proper connection and disconnection of trailers.
- Gain expertise in cargo handling and securing, ensuring loads are safely balanced and compliant with weight regulations.
- Recognize and respond to emergencies, including breakdowns and accident procedures, to minimize risks on the road.
- Understand how to navigate diverse driving conditions, such as steep grades, inclement weather, and heavy traffic, with safety as a priority.
- Learn the importance of maintaining a safe following distance and anticipating vehicle dynamics to avoid hazards.
- Familiarize yourself with hours-of-service regulations to ensure compliance with legal driving limits and prevent fatigue-related incidents.
- Understand proper use of safety systems, such as air brakes, emergency brakes, and on-board electronic logging devices.
- Articulate the importance of maintaining professionalism and fostering positive relationships with employers, clients, and the public.
- Discover best practices for record-keeping, including vehicle maintenance logs, driving hours, and compliance reports.
- Learn how to safely park, secure, and operate a Class A vehicle in various scenarios, from truck stops to loading docks.
