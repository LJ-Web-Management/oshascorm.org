### Course Objective

## CDL Entry Level Driver Training (ELDT) - Class B to A Upgrade

This CDL Entry Level Driver Training (ELDT) - Class B to A Upgrade course prepares you to take your commercial driving career to the next level! Tailored for those with a Class B license, this course equips you with the advanced knowledge and skills necessary to safely operate combination vehicles, ensuring the safety of your cargo, passengers, and everyone on the road.

- Understand Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations (FMCSR) and commercial vehicle safety requirements, ensuring you operate legally and responsibly with combination vehicles.
- Develop proficiency in pre-trip, en-route, and post-trip inspections, focusing on essential components like coupling devices, trailers, and brakes.
- Learn safe driving techniques for handling combination vehicles, including backing, turning, and navigating tight spaces with a trailer.
- Master the safe operation of combination vehicles, ensuring proper connection, disconnection, and handling of trailers during transit.
- Gain expertise in cargo handling and securing, ensuring that loads are properly balanced and comply with all relevant weight regulations.
- Recognize and respond to emergencies, including breakdowns and accident procedures, to minimize risks and protect everyone on the road.
- Learn how to navigate diverse driving conditions such as steep grades, inclement weather, and heavy traffic, prioritizing safety at all times.
- Understand the importance of maintaining a safe following distance and anticipating vehicle dynamics to avoid accidents.

- Understand the proper use of safety systems such as air brakes, emergency brakes, and onboard electronic logging devices.
- Recognize the importance of professionalism in your role, fostering positive relationships with employers, clients, and the public.
- Discover best practices for record-keeping, including vehicle maintenance logs, driving hours, and compliance reports.
- Learn how to safely park, secure, and operate a Class A vehicle in various scenarios, such as truck stops, loading docks, and more.
