## Course Overview

This course is designed to equip commercial drivers with the knowledge and skills necessary to safely transport passengers while complying with FMCSA and DOT regulations in **49 CFR Part 374**. The training includes general awareness, function-specific training, and emergency preparedness for passenger transportation.

### Key Topics Covered

- General awareness training on regulations to handle a passenger bus
- Specific requirements for transportation of passengers by highway
- Specific requirements for transportation of hazardous materials by highway
- Function-specific training including responsibilities of the driver
- Emergency preparedness and response training

### Who Should Enroll?

This training is ideal for driver-trainees seeking a CDL ELDT passenger (P) endorsement for the first time. From February 2022, FMCSA requires entry-level drivers to complete a compliant training program before taking the knowledge test for the P Endorsement.

### Licensing and Renewal

- No recurrent training is required; the P Endorsement does not expire
- Renewal is required with CDL expiration, typically every five years
- Some states may have additional security assessments or shorter CDL renewal cycles

### Course Objective

This CDL Entry Level Driver Training (ELDT) - Passenger Bus (P) Endorsement course prepares you to excel as a professional passenger bus driver! By the end, you'll be equipped to:

- Understand the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations (FMCSR) and passenger safety requirements, ensuring you operate legally and responsibly.
- Develop the skills needed to safely load and unload passengers, including individuals with disabilities or special needs.
- Learn effective techniques for passenger management, ensuring comfort and safety throughout the journey.
- Master pre-trip, en-route, and post-trip vehicle inspections, with a focus on passenger-specific components like emergency exits, seating, and handrails.
- Recognize and respond to emergencies, including evacuation procedures and safe handling of passenger-related incidents.
- Gain expertise in managing and securing passenger belongings, ensuring everything is safely stored during transit.
- Understand the regulations for transporting passengers across state lines, ensuring compliance with all legal requirements.
- Learn the importance of maintaining a safe following distance and anticipating passenger vehicle dynamics on the road.
- Navigate challenges like railroad crossings and traffic congestion while prioritizing passenger safety.
- Understand the use of on-board safety systems, such as seatbelts and emergency communication devices.
- Articulate the importance of maintaining professionalism and providing excellent customer service as a passenger bus driver.
- Discover the requirements for maintaining records related to passenger transport and safety inspections.
- Learn how to safely park and secure a passenger bus in various scenarios, minimizing risks during stops.
