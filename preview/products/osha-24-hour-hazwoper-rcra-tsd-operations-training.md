## OSHA 24 Hour HAZWOPER - RCRA TSD Operations Training

The **OSHA 24 Hour HAZWOPER - RCRA TSD Operations Training** course is designed for individuals seeking **initial HAZWOPER certification** to safely perform work in hazardous waste operations at treatment, storage, and disposal facilities (TSDFs). The course is fully compliant with OSHA regulations:

- 29 CFR 1910.120(p)(7)(i) – General Industry
- 29 CFR 1926.65(p)(7)(i) – Construction Industry

## Who Should Take This Course?

This training is intended for workers engaged in operations at TSDFs for hazardous waste, including handling, storage, and management of RCRA hazardous wastes. It is ideal for new employees or personnel requiring initial certification under OSHA HAZWOPER regulations.

## Course Objectives

- Understand hazardous waste types and associated risks.
- Learn proper safety and health procedures to perform tasks safely.
- Become familiar with employer Safety and Health Programs and OSHA standards.
- Gain practical knowledge to prevent accidents, exposure, and environmental harm.
- Prepare for safe operations at treatment, storage, and disposal facilities.

 Note: After completion of the 24-hour initial HAZWOPER training, TSDF workers are required to attend an **8-hour annual refresher course** to maintain certification and remain current with OSHA regulations.
