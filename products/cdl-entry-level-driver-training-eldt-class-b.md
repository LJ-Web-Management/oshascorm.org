### Course Objective

## CDL Entry Level Driver Training (ELDT) - Class B Drivers

This CDL Entry Level Driver Training (ELDT) - Class B Drivers course equips you with the knowledge and skills required to excel as a professional commercial driver. By the end, you'll be prepared to:

- Understand Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations (FMCSR) and commercial vehicle safety requirements, ensuring legal and responsible operation.
- Develop proficiency in pre-trip, en-route, and post-trip inspections, focusing on critical vehicle components like brakes, tires, and suspension systems.
- Learn safe driving techniques for handling single vehicles and smaller commercial vehicles, including turning, backing, and navigating tight spaces.
- Master the safe operation of straight trucks and buses, ensuring smooth handling and control on various types of roads.
- Gain expertise in cargo handling and securing, ensuring loads are safely balanced and compliant with weight regulations.
- Recognize and respond to emergencies, including breakdowns and accident procedures, to minimize risks on the road.
- Understand how to navigate diverse driving conditions, such as steep grades, inclement weather, and urban traffic, with safety as a priority.
- Learn the importance of maintaining a safe following distance and anticipating vehicle dynamics to avoid hazards.
- Familiarize yourself with hours-of-service regulations to ensure compliance with legal driving limits and prevent fatigue-related incidents.
- Understand proper use of safety systems, such as air brakes, emergency brakes, and on-board electronic logging devices.
- Articulate the importance of maintaining professionalism and fostering positive relationships with employers, clients, and the public.
- Discover best practices for record-keeping, including vehicle maintenance logs, driving hours, and compliance reports.
- Learn how to safely park, secure, and operate a Class B vehicle in various scenarios, from delivery sites to loading docks.
