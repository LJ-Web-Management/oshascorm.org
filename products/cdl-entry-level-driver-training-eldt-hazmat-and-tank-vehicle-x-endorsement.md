## CDL Entry Level Driver Training (ELDT) – Hazardous Materials & Tank Vehicle (X) Endorsement

Advance your commercial driving career with our **CDL Entry Level Driver Training (ELDT) – Hazardous Materials & Tank Vehicle (X) Endorsement** course. This training is designed for CDL holders seeking authorization to transport hazardous materials in tank vehicles, in full compliance with FMCSA and DOT regulations under **49 CFR Part 380**.

This course fulfills the FMCSA’s mandatory ELDT theory requirements for drivers applying for the X endorsement, combining both Hazardous Materials (H) and Tank Vehicle (N) endorsements.

## Why Choose Our CDL ELDT – X Endorsement?

- **FMCSA Compliant:** Meets all ELDT requirements effective February 7, 2022, required before testing for the Hazardous Materials and Tank Vehicle endorsements.
- **Convenient Online Training:** Complete the required theory training online at your own pace, anytime and anywhere.
- **Immediate FMCSA Reporting:** Your training record is submitted to the FMCSA Training Provider Registry (TPR) upon successful course completion.

## What You Will Learn

- Hazardous materials classifications, identification, and shipping requirements.
- Safe handling, loading, unloading, and securement of hazardous materials.
- Tank vehicle operation principles, including surge, baffling, and vehicle stability.
- Emergency response procedures and incident reporting requirements.
- Hazard communication, placarding, and regulatory compliance.

## Who Needs This Course?

- **CDL Holders:** Drivers seeking the Hazardous Materials and Tank Vehicle (X) endorsement.
- **Fuel & Chemical Transport Drivers:** Individuals transporting liquids, gases, or hazardous substances.
- **Fleet Operators & Employers:** Organizations requiring compliant, endorsement-ready drivers.

  **Note:** Completion of this ELDT theory course is required before taking the knowledge test for the Hazardous Materials and Tank Vehicle (X) Endorsement.

### Course Objective

Upon successful completion of this CDL Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) – Hazardous Materials & Tank Vehicle (X) Endorsement course, participants will be able to:

- Understand the importance of FMCSA regulations and the DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations (HMR) when applying for a CDL X endorsement
- Identify basic hazardous materials classifications, applicable hazardous materials regulations, and shipping paper requirements
- Recognize hazardous materials marking, labeling, and placarding requirements, and explain emergency response information and shipper responsibilities
- Demonstrate core competencies for the safe transportation of hazardous materials
- Follow proper procedures for reporting hazardous materials incidents and accurately complete and submit incident reports
- Operate hazardous materials vehicles safely at railroad crossings and in tunnels
- Apply correct loading and unloading procedures, including segregation, securement, and restrictions involving foodstuffs
- Identify requirements for transporting passengers and property with hazardous materials, including permitted and prohibited materials and quantities
- Understand special regulatory requirements for transporting hazardous materials in bulk packages
- Demonstrate proper inspection, safe operation, and loading procedures for tank vehicles in compliance with applicable standards
- Follow FMCSA regulations and safe vehicle operation procedures, including special precautions related to fire hazards
- Respond effectively to hazardous materials emergencies and post-incident operations, prioritizing the protection of people, property, and the environment
- Follow safe fueling procedures for vehicles transporting hazardous materials
- Conduct proper pre-trip inspections and parking inspections of vehicle tires
- Follow approved routing procedures for radioactive and non-radioactive hazardous materials
- Understand communication, attendance, parking, and procedural requirements for hazardous materials requiring a Hazardous Materials Safety Permit
