### What You Will Learn

- Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations (FMCSR) for school bus operations
- Emergency Procedures including accidents, medical emergencies, or hijackings
- Route Planning strategies for safe and timely student transport

- Handling special safety requirements and on-road decision-making

### Licensing Requirements

- FMCSA Mandate: Entry-level drivers must complete a compliant training program before taking the knowledge test for the S Endorsement (since February 2022)
- No Recurrent Training: The school bus ELDT endorsement does not expire but must be renewed when CDL expires (typically every five years)
- State Requirements: Some states may have additional security assessments or shorter CDL renewal cycles
