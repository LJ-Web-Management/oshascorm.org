## Carrier Requirements: Highway

Function-specific training for personnel involved in the transport of hazardous materials by private, common, and contract motor carriers, covering packaging, loading, and unloading operations.

## Course Overview

The **DOT Hazmat Carrier Requirements: Highway** course is designed for hazmat employers and employees engaged in transportation of hazardous materials by motor carriers. This includes drivers transporting hazardous materials and personnel who package, load, and unload cargo for shipment by highway. The course ensures compliance with DOT regulations and promotes safe highway transport practices.

### Who should take this course?

- Drivers transporting hazardous materials via motor carriers
- Personnel responsible for packaging, loading, and unloading hazardous materials
- Safety and compliance officers overseeing highway hazmat operations
- Shippers coordinating highway transportation of hazardous materials

### Learning Objectives

- Understand carrier responsibilities and highway-specific DOT regulations
- Properly package, label, and load hazardous materials for highway transport
- Apply safe unloading and handling procedures
- Maintain required documentation and compliance records
- Recognize emergency response measures for highway hazmat incidents

### Course Outline

1. DOT hazmat regulations applicable to highway transportation
2. Carrier duties and responsibilities
3. Packaging, labeling, and placarding requirements for highway transport
4. Loading, unloading, and safe handling procedures
5. Documentation and compliance records
6. Emergency response procedures

### Prerequisites

Learners should have basic DOT hazmat knowledge, preferably from DOT Hazmat Transportation: General Awareness Training.

### Course Objective

This DOT Hazmat Carrier Requirements: Highway Training course equips you with the knowledge and skills to navigate the regulations for transporting hazardous materials by road. Upon completion, you'll be able to:

- Gain a strong understanding of the regulatory framework governing the transport of hazardous materials by highway, ensuring compliance with federal guidelines.
- Summarize the training requirements for both motor vehicle operators and cargo tank operators, ensuring everyone involved possesses the necessary qualifications.
- Explain the critical role of shipping papers and certifications for transporting hazardous materials by highway, promoting accurate documentation and clear communication.
- Understand the requirements for safely segregating and stowing hazardous materials during transport, minimizing the risk of accidents and incidents.
- Grasp both the general and specific loading and unloading requirements for motor vehicles and cargo tanks, ensuring safe and compliant handling practices.
- Understand the reporting protocols for incidents involving hazardous materials, ensuring timely communication with authorities in case of an accident.
- Describe key strategies for managing security risks associated with transporting hazardous materials, contributing to a safe and secure transportation environment.
