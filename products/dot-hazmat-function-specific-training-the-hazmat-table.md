## Course Overview

The **DOT Hazmat Function Specific — The Hazmat Table (HMT)** course provides an in-depth look at the Hazardous Materials Table, the central reference for complying with the United States Department of Transportation Hazardous Materials Regulations (HMR). The HMT identifies requirements for identification, packaging, labeling, marking, placarding, and other mode-specific requirements.

### Who should take this course?

- Regulatory compliance staff and safety officers
- Shippers preparing hazardous materials for transport
- Packaging and labeling personnel who reference the HMT
- Supervisors and trainers responsible for hazmat procedures

### Learning Objectives

- Interpret and apply HMT entries to identify shipping requirements.
- Understand column entries such as proper shipping name, hazard class, ID number, packing group, label/placard requirements, and special provisions.
- Determine mode-specific requirements and exceptions for various transport modes.
- Use the HMT to select appropriate packaging, labeling, marking, and placarding for shipments.
- Maintain records and documentation required by the HMR using HMT guidance.

### Course Outline

1. Introduction to the HMT & regulatory context (HMR)
2. Detailed review of HMT columns and entries
3. Interpreting special provisions and exceptions
4. Mode-specific considerations (air, vessel, rail, highway)
5. Practical exercises: reading HMT entries and completing shipping descriptions
6. Assessment and certification

### Prerequisites

Learners should have completed DOT Hazmat Transportation: General Awareness Training or have equivalent hazmat basics prior to this function-specific module.
