## HIPAA General Awareness Training

**Protecting patient data** is essential — HIPAA violations can lead to lawsuits, loss of trust, and harm to patients. Our HIPAA General Awareness course teaches staff how to safeguard Protected Health Information (PHI) by following the Privacy and Security Rules in **45 CFR Parts 160, 162, and 164**. Delivered online and device-friendly, the course mixes policy, practical tips, and quizzes to build confidence and compliance.

## Course Overview

Designed for clinical and non-clinical staff, IT, vendors, and managers, this short online course explains HIPAA basics, common risks, and everyday steps to keep PHI secure. It’s practical, concise, and suitable for onboarding or annual refreshers.

### Why choose our HIPAA training?

 **Regulation-focused** — Aligns with federal requirements in *45 CFR Parts 160, 162, and 164*. **Practical & role-based** — Real workplace scenarios for providers, admin staff, IT, vendors, and managers. **Flexible delivery** — Online, device-friendly modules with quizzes and a downloadable certificate on completion. **Designed by compliance pros** — Clear policy guidance plus hands-on steps to prevent breaches.

### What you will learn

- Why HIPAA exists and each staff role in protecting PHI
- Who is a covered entity vs. a business associate
- Practical safeguards for paper, screens, and mobile devices
- Privacy Rule basics and permitted disclosures without consent
- Security Rule essentials: encryption, access control, updates
- Common mistakes (wrong recipient, weak passwords, lost devices) and how to avoid them
- Immediate steps for suspected breaches and notification timelines
- Vendor vetting, contracts, and monitoring third-party access
- Secure email, encrypted cloud storage, and audit trail best practices

### Who needs this course?

- Healthcare providers (doctors, nurses, therapists)
- Administrative staff (billing, scheduling, front desk)
- IT personnel managing EHRs and systems
- Third-party vendors and business associates
- Compliance officers and HR staff
- New hires and managers/executives overseeing PHI

### Course Objective

- Identify key environmental laws applicable to workers at hazardous waste sites.
- Understand how supervisor training aligns with HAZWOPER training and refresher requirements.
- Outline management leadership principles and supervisory responsibilities.
- Describe the essential components of a comprehensive Safety and Health Program.
- Define industrial hygiene and its four core functions.
- Explain the basics of a Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Program and how to choose the right PPE for specific worksites.
- Recognize the importance of a Heat Illness Prevention Plan and its key elements.
- Understand the role of a Spill Containment Program in handling hazardous material spills.
