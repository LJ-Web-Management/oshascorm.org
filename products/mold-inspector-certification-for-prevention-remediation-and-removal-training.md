### Overview

## Mold Inspector Certification: Prevention, Remediation, and Removal

Learn to conduct thorough and compliant mold inspections while following EPA, OSHA, and IAC2 standards.

## Course Overview

This California mold inspector training emphasizes scientific understanding and hands-on skills to prepare students to identify visible and hidden mold growth, assess moisture conditions, and evaluate potential health and structural risks tied to indoor air quality. Learners gain the knowledge to conduct defensible mold assessments compliant with industry standards.

### Key Training Features

- Conduct comprehensive mold evaluations using visual and sampling techniques
- Identify moisture sources, humidity issues, and building-science factors promoting mold growth
- Apply the IAC2 Standards of Practice for consistent inspection procedures
- Use professional sampling equipment and interpret laboratory results accurately
- Recommend remediation and prevention strategies based on EPA and OSHA best practices

### Regulatory & Industry Standards

This training integrates guidance from **EPA**, **OSHA**, and **IAC2 Standards of Practice** to ensure safe, compliant inspections. Emphasis is placed on documentation, ethical procedures, and environmental health and safety (EHS) principles. Participants learn to manage mold-related hazards while protecting occupants and personnel during inspection and remediation activities.

### Who Should Enroll?

- Home inspectors and property assessors expanding service capabilities
- Environmental health and safety professionals specializing in indoor air quality
- Building managers, contractors, and remediation specialists overseeing property maintenance
- Public health and housing professionals involved in environmental compliance and risk assessment

### Course Objective

By the end of this course, you'll be able to:

- Understand what mold is, its properties, and potential health risks from exposure.
- Learn the IAC2 Mold Inspection Standards and how to conduct thorough visual inspections for mold in buildings.
- Discover the conditions that encourage mold growth and how to spot it with a trained eye.
- Utilize the IAC2 Mold Sampling Decision Chart to determine if sampling is necessary.
- Learn how to choose and use the right tools, equipment, and protective gear for a safe mold inspection.
- Develop your skills in hypothesis building and gain valuable knowledge about building science, moisture intrusion, and sampling techniques.
- Learn effective methods for documenting your mold inspection work.
- Understand how to interpret laboratory results in the context of mold inspections.
- Gain knowledge of best practices and techniques for mold remediation.
- Develop your report writing skills to effectively communicate mold inspection findings.
- Learn preventive measures and strategies to stop mold growth in its tracks before it becomes a problem.
