## Mold Prevention and Remediation Awareness Training

The **Mold Prevention and Remediation Awareness Training** is an awareness-level course designed to introduce best practices for mold prevention, remediation, and cleanup. The course also emphasizes measures required to protect the health of building occupants and workers involved in mold remediation activities.

### Course Overview

Mold can pose significant health risks, especially in poorly ventilated or moisture-prone areas. This training equips learners with practical knowledge about identifying mold growth, preventing mold-related issues, and safely managing remediation projects. Participants will learn to implement effective strategies that minimize exposure and maintain a safe environment for all.

### Who Can Benefit from This Training?

- Building managers responsible for facility maintenance and safety
- Custodians and maintenance personnel handling building hygiene
- Contractors involved in remediation or construction projects in mold-prone areas
- Industrial hygienists and environmental health & safety professionals managing mold and moisture concerns
- General public seeking awareness on mold prevention and cleanup practices

### Key Learning Outcomes

- Understand common sources and types of mold in indoor environments
- Learn best practices for preventing mold growth in buildings
- Recognize safe methods for mold remediation and cleanup
- Understand health risks associated with mold exposure for workers and occupants
- Identify steps to protect personnel and building occupants during remediation
- Stay informed on evolving guidelines and scientific research regarding mold safety

### Course Objective

By the end of this course, you'll be able to:

- Understand what mold is, where it thrives indoors, and why it's a concern in our buildings.
- Define terms like Building-Related Illnesses and Sick Building Syndrome to understand potential health effects of mold exposure.
- Learn about the health risks associated with breathing in mold spores.
- Discover specific practices for keeping buildings, crawl spaces, HVAC systems, and high-moisture areas mold-free.
- Learn about the equipment used to assess and remediate mold problems.
- Understand the pros and cons of mold sampling to determine if it's necessary.
- Describe the methods for cleaning up mold and why biocides are not a preferred solution.
- Learn about different types of personal protective equipment (PPE) to minimize exposure to mold and dust.
- Understand area-specific remediation guidelines, including special considerations for HVAC systems and confined spaces.
