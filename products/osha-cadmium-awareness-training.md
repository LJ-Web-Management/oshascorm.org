## What is Cadmium?

Cadmium is a toxic heavy metal commonly found in industrial materials and processes such as metal plating, battery manufacturing, welding, cutting, and pigment production. Exposure to cadmium—primarily through inhalation or ingestion—can lead to serious health effects. Common risks include:

- Kidney damage
- Lung disease and respiratory issues
- Bone damage and osteoporosis
- Cancer and other long-term health effects

## Employer Responsibilities

Employers are required to protect employees from cadmium exposure by implementing OSHA-compliant safety programs. Employers must ensure that employees:

- Receive cadmium awareness training from a qualified individual
- Understand exposure limits and health risks associated with cadmium
- Follow proper work practices and control measures
- Use appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE)
