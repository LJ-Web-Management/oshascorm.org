## OSHA Driving Safety Training

The OSHA Driving Safety Training course is designed to promote safe driving practices for employees who operate vehicles as part of their job duties. Motor vehicle incidents remain one of the leading causes of workplace injuries and fatalities. This training focuses on risk prevention, defensive driving strategies, and employer responsibilities to help organizations maintain safe driving operations in compliance with OSHA’s workplace safety principles and applicable transportation safety guidelines.

Participants will learn safe driving techniques, hazard recognition, vehicle inspection procedures, distracted driving prevention, and emergency response actions. The course also emphasizes defensive driving, situational awareness, and best practices for reducing road-related workplace incidents.

### Why This Course Is Important

Work-related driving exposes employees to significant risks. Proper training helps reduce accidents, protect employees, and ensure organizations maintain a strong safety culture. This course is important because:

- Motor vehicle accidents are a leading cause of workplace injuries and fatalities.
- Employees who drive for work must understand safe driving practices and hazard awareness.
- Employers have a responsibility to minimize risks associated with occupational driving.
- Training improves driver awareness, decision-making, and accident prevention.
- Safe driving practices help protect employees, the public, and company assets.

### Who Should Take This Course?

This driving safety training is ideal for:

- Employees who drive company vehicles or personal vehicles for work purposes.
- Delivery drivers, transportation personnel, and field service technicians.
- Construction and utility workers traveling between job sites.
- Sales representatives and mobile workforce employees.
- Supervisors and safety managers responsible for fleet safety programs.
- Employers seeking to improve workplace driving safety and reduce accident risks.

### What This Course Covers

The training provides a comprehensive understanding of workplace driving safety, including:

- Fundamentals of defensive driving and safe vehicle operation.
- Common causes of workplace driving accidents and how to prevent them.
- Vehicle inspection and basic maintenance checks before driving.
- Managing hazards such as weather conditions, road environments, and traffic risks.
- Preventing distracted driving and fatigue-related incidents.
- Safe following distances, speed management, and situational awareness.
- Communication and awareness around pedestrians and other vehicles.
- Accident response procedures and reporting requirements.
- Employer responsibilities for driver safety programs.
- Best practices for maintaining a safe driving culture in the workplace.

### Course Objective

By taking OSHA Driving Safety Training course,You'll be able to:

- Explain occupational driving as a workplace hazard and its impact on employee safety.
- Describe OSHA regulatory requirements related to workplace driving safety.
- Identify employer and employee responsibilities under the OSH Act.
- Explain vehicle characteristics and operational limitations.
- Interpret essential vehicle instruments, controls, and safety systems.
- Define and distinguish occupational driving hazards such as distracted driving, impaired driving, aggressive driving, and drowsy driving.
- Explain defensive driving principles including safe following distance, lane changes, and intersection navigation.
- Conduct pre-shift vehicle inspections, including visual inspections, operational checks, and safety system checks.
- Implement safe driving practices during extreme weather conditions.
- Explain methods to prevent preventable collisions through hazard anticipation and situational awareness.
- Describe how to handle emergencies and changing road conditions to prevent potential incidents.
