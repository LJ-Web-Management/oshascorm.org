## Pandemic Awareness, Preparedness & Response Training

Practical guidance for businesses on planning, response, and protecting workforce health during a pandemic.

## Course Overview

This course equips employers and staff with the knowledge to understand the impact of a pandemic on business operations, workforce health, and the broader community. It explains the essential elements of a business continuity plan and outlines hazard controls and practical measures to protect employees and maintain critical operations.

### Why this training matters

While pandemics are infrequent, their effects can be widespread and long-lasting. The COVID-19 pandemic demonstrated how rapidly a pathogen can disrupt normal business activity. Preparing in advance ensures a faster, more effective response that reduces risk to employees and helps sustain business continuity.

### Who should take this course?

- Business leaders and continuity planners
- HR and occupational health professionals
- Facilities and operations managers
- Supervisors and frontline staff
- Safety and emergency response teams

### Learning Objectives

- Describe the public health and business impacts of a pandemic.
- Outline the components of an effective business continuity plan for pandemics.
- Identify engineering, administrative, and PPE controls to reduce transmission risk.
- Develop communication, response, and recovery procedures for workforce safety.
- Plan for workforce resilience, remote work, and critical operations continuity.

### Course Outline

1. Introduction to pandemics: definitions and historical examples
2. Public health principles and transmission dynamics
3. Business continuity planning: risk assessment and critical functions
4. Hazard controls: engineering, administrative, and PPE strategies
5. Workforce protection: screening, testing, vaccination policies, and mental health support
6. Communication and stakeholder engagement during a pandemic
7. Recovery, post-event evaluation, and continuous improvement
8. Exercises, scenario planning, and template business continuity plans

### Course Objective

This Pandemic Awareness, Preparedness, and Response Training equips you to confidently navigate a global health crisis. Upon completion, you'll be able to:

- Grasp the far-reaching effects of pandemics on businesses, industries, and communities.
- Learn about Non-Pharmaceutical Interventions (NPIs) and their importance in protecting your workplace from outbreaks.
- Differentiate between a regular business continuity plan and a specialized infectious disease preparedness and response plan.
- Explore the specific strategies and actions for all three phases of an infectious disease preparedness and response plan.
- Understand OSHA's occupational risk pyramid and utilize its hazard controls and intervention strategies for different exposure groups.
- Navigate OSHA standards and recordkeeping requirements specific to COVID-19.
- Summarize the guidelines for individuals and employers during the three phases of resuming work operations post-pandemic.
- Discuss emerging work trends that may shape businesses and individuals in a post-pandemic world.
