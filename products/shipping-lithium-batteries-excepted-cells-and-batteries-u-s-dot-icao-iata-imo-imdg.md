### Overview

## Shipping Lithium Batteries: Excepted Cells and Batteries

In-depth training on shipping smaller or excepted lithium cells and batteries safely via highway, rail, air, and water in compliance with DOT, IATA, and IMDG regulations.

## Course Overview

This course encompasses shipping guidelines for "excepted" lithium cells and batteries, covering:

- Stand-alone lithium cells or batteries
- Lithium cells or batteries packed with equipment
- Lithium cells or batteries contained in equipment

The training ensures employees understand general awareness and function-specific requirements for transportation. All personnel whose job function directly affects the safe transport of hazardous materials must complete training within 90 days of employment or a change in job function.

### Learning Objectives

- Understand applicable DOT HMR, IATA DGR, and IMDG regulations
- Identify different types of lithium batteries and cells for shipping
- Apply proper packaging, labeling, and documentation procedures
- Ensure safe handling and transportation by highway, rail, air, and water
- Comply with training requirements and maintain regulatory records

### Course Objective

This Shipping Lithium Batteries: Excepted Cells and Batteries Training course equips you with the knowledge and skills to navigate the regulations for transporting smaller, less hazardous lithium batteries. Upon completion, you'll be able to:

- Gain a comprehensive understanding of the various regulations governing the shipment of excepted lithium cells and batteries by air, land, and sea (U.S. DOT, IATA, IMDG Code).
- Understand the specific training mandates for personnel handling hazardous materials, ensuring your team is compliant.
- Learn how to locate crucial information on the Hazardous Materials Table (HMT) for accurate inclusion on shipping documents.
- Grasp the essential requirements for the safe packaging, marking, and labeling of excepted lithium batteries.
- Develop a strong understanding of the potential hazards associated with handling, storing, and transporting lithium cells and batteries.
- Discover best practices for the safe use and storage of excepted lithium batteries, minimizing risks throughout their lifecycle.
- Master the specific shipping guidelines for excepted lithium batteries, applicable to separate shipments, equipment packing, and contained equipment scenarios.
- Understand the exemptions for shipping low production runs and prototypes of excepted lithium batteries.
- Learn the proper protocols for the safe disposal of excepted lithium cells and batteries, ensuring environmental responsibility.
- Identify the different types of extinguishing agents most effective in combating fires caused by lithium metal and lithium-ion batteries (though less likely with excepted batteries).
